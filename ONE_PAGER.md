# Re-Level: Stop getting locked out of your own homework.

## 🎯 Who we built this for

**Persona:** Sarah, a 10th-grade ESL (English as a Second Language) student. 

Sarah is bright and capable, but her AP History textbook is written with dense, archaic jargon that takes her hours to decipher. When she uses standard translation apps like Google Translate, she gets literal word-for-word translations that lose the historical context, leaving her frustrated and exhausted. She just wants to understand the material without fighting the vocabulary.

## 🚧 What barrier it removes

**The Comprehension Barrier.** 

Information should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their current reading level or primary language. Re-Level removes the friction of academic jargon. It allows students to focus entirely on *learning the core concepts* rather than wasting time *decoding the vocabulary*.

## ⚙️ How it works (in 3 steps)

1. **Snap or Paste:** Take a photo of any complex textbook passage or worksheet. Our 100% client-side OCR instantly and securely extracts the text.
2. **Tune your Level:** Select your native language and use a simple slider to dial in your exact reading level (e.g., Grade 6 or ESL Intermediate).
3. **Understand Instantly:** Re-Level instantly rewrites the text. It meticulously preserves all factual accuracy and educational context, but delivers it in simplified language you actually understand. (You can even click "Read Aloud" to hear it spoken!)
